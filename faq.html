Planning a painting project? Here’s what homeowners often want to know before getting started.

How much does interior painting cost?

Interior painting is usually priced based on the amount of surface area, wall condition, preparation, number of coats, trim and ceilings, and the products selected. A simple repaint of sound walls generally costs less than a room requiring extensive patching, stain blocking or detailed trim work. Empty rooms are often faster to work in than fully furnished spaces. Rather than quote a misleading flat price per room, New Leaf provides an estimate based on the actual surfaces and work involved.

Do I need to move furniture before painting?

It helps to remove small furniture, wall décor, fragile items, electronics and personal belongings before work begins. Larger pieces may be moved toward the center of the room when space permits and protected with appropriate coverings. We also protect floors and nearby surfaces during painting. The amount of moving and preparation needed depends on the room layout, furniture size and agreed project scope; let us know about anything especially heavy or delicate.

How long will my painting project take?

A straightforward single-room repaint may take a day or two, while multiple rooms, extensive repairs, detailed trim or cabinet work can take several days or longer. Proper preparation, primer, multiple coats and drying or curing time all affect the schedule. Humidity and temperature can also slow drying. We'll provide a more specific timeline once we know the scope and existing conditions.

Do you repair drywall before painting?

Yes. Small nail holes, dents and minor cracks can often be filled, sanded and primed before painting. Larger holes or damaged sections may require new drywall, joint tape and multiple coats of compound. Some cracks or stains may point to movement or moisture problems that should be addressed before cosmetic repairs. The right approach depends on what caused the damage and how extensive it is.

Can you paint kitchen cabinets instead of replacing them?

Absolutely. If your existing cabinets are structurally sound and you like the current layout, professional cabinet painting or refinishing can be a much more affordable way to refresh your kitchen than buying and installing new cabinetry. A quality result requires thorough cleaning and degreasing, careful surface preparation, appropriate bonding primer and durable cabinet-grade finishes—not simply brushing wall paint onto the doors. Some wood grain may remain visible depending on the material and desired finish. Peeling laminate, significant water damage, failing cabinet boxes or a layout you want to change may make replacement a better option. The best choice and actual savings depend on cabinet condition, materials, size and finish expectations.

When is the best time to schedule exterior painting in Maine?

Late spring through early fall is generally the most practical exterior painting season in Southern Maine, but the weather matters more than the calendar. Many exterior coatings require air and surface temperatures of at least about 50°F during application and drying; some products are rated for lower temperatures, so the manufacturer's instructions always take priority. Surfaces must be dry, and painting should be avoided during rain, heavy dew, frost, or when temperatures are expected to fall below the product's limits before the coating cures. High humidity, strong direct sun and very hot siding can also affect application. Because Maine weather changes quickly, exterior schedules may shift to protect the quality of the finish.

Can I schedule painting around a home sale or move?

Often, yes—especially when we know the deadline early. Painting before furniture arrives can make access and floor protection easier, while pre-listing projects may benefit from prioritizing visible walls, trim and repairs. Fresh coatings also need the drying and curing time recommended by the manufacturer before heavy use or cleaning. Share your move, listing or closing date with us; the schedule we can commit to depends on project size, preparation needs and existing bookings.
