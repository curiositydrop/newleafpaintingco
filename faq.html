Planning a painting project? Here’s what homeowners often want to know before getting started.

How much does interior painting cost?

Pricing depends on room size, surface condition, preparation, trim and the type of finish. We provide a project-specific estimate after learning about your space.

Do I need to move furniture before painting?

Clearing small items and valuables helps. We can discuss larger furniture, access and protection needs during your estimate so everyone knows what to expect.

How long will my painting project take?

Timing depends on preparation, drying conditions, number of coats and project size. We’ll discuss a realistic schedule before work begins.

Do you repair drywall before painting?

Yes. We offer drywall patching and finish work as part of many interior projects. Share any damage when requesting an estimate.

Can you paint kitchen cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes. Cabinet refinishing can refresh an existing kitchen. We’ll review cabinet condition and the desired finish before recommending an approach.

When is the best time to schedule exterior painting in Maine?

Exterior painting depends on weather, temperatures, moisture and the coating manufacturer’s requirements. We schedule around suitable conditions rather than forcing a job into bad weather.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Tell us your town, project type and details using the estimate request form. We’ll follow up to arrange an appointment.

What areas do you serve?

New Leaf works across Southern Maine. Include your town in the estimate request so we can confirm availability for your location.

Do you work with real estate agents and property managers?

Yes. We can discuss pre-listing painting, cosmetic updates, turnover work and repair punch lists with the agent or property owner.

Can I schedule painting around a home sale or move?

We understand deadlines matter. Share your listing, closing or move date up front; scheduling depends on the scope and our availability.
