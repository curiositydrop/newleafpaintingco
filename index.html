Painting questions

How much does it cost to paint a room in Southern Maine?

Standard interior painting includes labor, paint, light prep, and two coats. Walls are $2.05 per square foot of gross wall area: 2 × (length + width) × wall height, without deducting ordinary doors and windows. A flat ceiling is $2.25 per square foot. A 12 × 12 foot bedroom with 8 foot walls is $787.20 for the walls, or $1,111.20 with the ceiling. Forty-five feet of standard baseboard at $4.20 per foot adds $189.00, for $1,300.20. Door and window casing is also $4.20 per linear foot. Door slabs, repairs, and specialty coatings are quoted after Mike reviews photos. See the pricing page or send a photo estimate.

Can I get a painting estimate by sending photos?

Yes. Get a Photo Estimate, add each room’s photos and measurements, and Mike will email a painting estimate within 24–48 hours. That estimate is based on the photos, measurements, and work you request. If the scope or condition is different in person, Mike will review any price change with you before work begins.
