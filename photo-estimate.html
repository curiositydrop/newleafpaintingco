Painting questions

How much does it cost to paint a room in Southern Maine?

Standard interior painting includes labor, paint, light prep, and two coats. Walls are $2.05 per square foot of gross wall area: 2 × (length + width) × wall height. A ceiling painted on its own is $2.25 per square foot, or $1.75 per square foot when the walls in that room are painted too. A 12 × 12 foot bedroom with 8 foot walls is $787.20 for the walls, or $1,039.20 with the ceiling. Forty-five feet of baseboard at $4.20 per foot adds $189.00, for $1,228.20. The total on this form is an estimate until we review your photos.
